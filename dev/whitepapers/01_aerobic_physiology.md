## Topics + primary sources

### 1. Mitochondrial biogenesis
- Little JP et al. 2010, J Physiol 588(6):1011-1022 — HIIT drives citrate synthase, COXII/IV, TFAM, PGC-1α +25% in 2 weeks (n=7)
- Perry CG et al. 2010, Appl Physiol Nutr Metab 35(6):837-844 — 7 weeks 3×/wk HIIT: CS/β-HAD/COXIV +28-36%
- Konopka AR et al. 2014, J Gerontol A 69(4):371-378 — 12 wks progressive cycling: PGC-1α +55-62%, CS +65-102%, β-HAD +397-435%, COXIV +80-126%; no age difference
- Granata C et al. 2016, FASEB J 30(10):3413-3423 — reduced volume reversed most mitochondrial adaptations in 2 weeks
- Bishop DJ et al. 2019, Physiology 34(1):56-70 — pathway synthesis (AMPK, Ca²⁺/CaMKII, p38 MAPK, ROS/RNS)
- Egan B, Zierath JR 2013, Cell Metab 17(2):162-184 — molecular exercise-adaptation review

### 2. Capillary density
- Andersen P, Henriksson J 1977, J Physiol 270(3):677-690 — foundational, VO₂max +16%, capillary density +20% in 8 weeks
- Cocks M et al. 2013, J Physiol 591(3):641-656 — SIT and MICT both produce comparable c/f ratio + eNOS gains
- 2025 meta-regression, PMC11787188 — ET +13±3% cap density; SIT non-significant

### 3. Stroke volume + cardiac remodeling

- Baggish AL, Wood MJ 2011, Circulation 123(23):2723-2735 — endurance drives eccentric LV hypertrophy; strength drives concentric

## Practical constraints for Engine Builder session choices

- Weekly minimum stimulus for measurable adaptation: 3 sessions/week, ≥2 at 30+ min Z2, plus 1 higher-intensity session per week
- Cardiac output requires intensity — Z2 alone won't move SV meaningfully; include Norwegian 4×4 or similar (Helgerud 2007; Wisløff 2007)
- Zone determination: use HUNT (211 − 0.64 × age) as HRmax default; use %HRR (Karvonen) not %HRmax for zone floors
- Maintenance dose during subsequent strength focus: ≥2 aerobic sessions/week, intensity preserved (Mujika & Padilla 2000)
- Don't label non-responders until intensity has been escalated + ≥2 baselines exist (Ross 2015; Hecksteden 2015)
- Track threshold pace/power and submax HR, not VO2max, as primary progression metric (Joyner & Coyle 2008)
