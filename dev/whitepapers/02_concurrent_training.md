# Concurrent Training — Primary Source Evidence Base

*Source report for the trainer-parity defense and Engine Builder's "concurrent strength" guidance. 35+ primary studies with effect sizes.*

## The interference-effect literature — resolved by dose

### Foundational + molecular
- Hickson RC 1980, Eur J Appl Physiol 45:255-263 — the original 10-week study; strength plateaued at week 7 in the concurrent group (design flaws acknowledged)
- Atherton PJ et al. 2005, FASEB J 19:786-788 — AMPK-PKB switch: low-freq stim raises AMPK, high-freq stim raises PKB/mTOR
- Coffey VG, Hawley JA 2007, Sports Med 37:737-763 — foundational integrative review
- Baar K 2014, Sports Med 44(S2):S117-S125 — 3-hour AMPK window post-endurance; mTORC1 sensitised 18-24 h post-lifting
- Fyfe JJ, Bishop DJ, Stepto NK 2014, Sports Med 44:743-762 — the definitive review of the mechanism and its modulators

### Modern meta-analyses reconciling the story
- Wilson JM et al. 2012, J Strength Cond Res 26:2293-2307 — 21 studies, 422 ES: concurrent reduced strength ES by ~18%, hypertrophy by ~31%, power by ~40%. Frequency matters (r = −0.26 to −0.35); duration matters (r = −0.29 to −0.75). Running > cycling for interference.
- Murach KA, Bagley JR 2016, Sports Med 46:1029-1039 — contrary evidence: hypertrophy not consistently reduced when protocols are ecologically valid
- Schumann M et al. 2022, Sports Med 52:601-612 — updated meta-analysis, 43 studies. **Maximal strength SMD = -0.06 (n.s.). Hypertrophy SMD = -0.01 (n.s.). Explosive strength SMD = -0.28 (p=0.007).** The modern reconciliation: dose matters; explosive strength / power is the real casualty.

### Session order + separation
- Eddens L et al. 2018, Sports Med 48:177-188 — resistance-before-endurance produced +6.91% lower-body dynamic strength gain vs endurance-first (p=0.006)
- Robineau J et al. 2016, J Strength Cond Res 30:672-683 — 7 weeks in rugby players, 4 groups (C-0h, C-6h, C-24h, strength-only). Bench + half-squat gains significantly lower in C-0h. **6h separation preserves strength; 24h optimizes VO2max.** Clear dose-response.

### Modality
- Wilson JM et al. 2012 (as above) — running produces more interference than cycling; cycling near-zero interference
- Doma K et al. 2019, Sports Med 49:669-682 — bidirectional damage: running-induced damage impairs subsequent squat/deadlift force for 24-48 h
- Berryman N et al. 2018, IJSPP 13:57-64 — cycling shows cleanest bidirectional compatibility with strength

### Intensity vs frequency (counterintuitive result)
- Fyfe JJ et al. 2016, Front Physiol 7:487 — 8 weeks: HIT+RT and MICT+RT produced SAME strength decrement (~0.9% difference). **Endurance INTENSITY does not mediate interference — endurance VOLUME does.**
- Petré H et al. 2018, J Sports Sci Med 17:167-173 — highly trained lifters: HIIT vs continuous produced same squat gains

### Protein / carb / nutrition
- Aragon AA, Schoenfeld BJ 2013, JISSN 10:5 — "anabolic window" is 4-6 h, not 30 min
- Morton RW et al. 2018, BJSM 52:376-384 — meta-analysis: no further hypertrophy benefit above 1.62 g/kg/day protein
- Jäger R et al. 2017, JISSN 14:20 — ISSN position stand: 1.4-2.0 g/kg/day for exercising individuals
- Bartlett JD et al. 2015, Eur J Sport Sci 15:3-12 — "train low, compete high" — low-carb availability amplifies AMPK/PGC-1α/p53
- Impey SG et al. 2018, Sports Med 48:1031-1048 — "Fuel for the Work Required" framework

### Individual variation in interference
- Bouchard C et al. 1999 HERITAGE — heritability of VO2max response = 47%; between-family variance 2.5× within-family
- Bouchard C et al. 2011, JAP 110:1160-1170 — 21-SNP model explains 49% of variance
- Ross R et al. 2015 Mayo Clin Proc — non-response at 50% intensity drops to 0% at 75%; often a dose issue, not a genotype

### CrossFit + HYROX specific
- Butcher SJ et al. 2015, Open Access J Sports Med 6:241-247 — CrossFit Total is strongest predictor of Fran/Grace times
- Meyer J, Morrison J, Zuniga J 2017, Workplace Health Saf 65:612-618 — CrossFit systematic review: VO2max, body comp, strength all improve concurrently in ~10 wk protocols
- Feito Y et al. 2018, Sports 6:76 — 16 weeks HIFT: strength AND VO2max improved concurrently
- Brandt K et al. 2025, Front Physiol 16:1519240 — first HYROX physiological profiling: VO2max is strongest performance predictor; grip strength / muscle mass NOT reliable predictors

## Effect-size summary — engine builder concurrent strength claims

Anchoring the "RPE ≤ 7 maintenance, 2-3 lifts/wk, no PR chasing" rules:

- Maximal strength during 8-wk aerobic block: no meaningful loss expected (Schumann 2022 SMD -0.06)
- Explosive strength during 8-wk aerobic block: **expect small decrement** (SMD -0.28, ~28% relative to strength-only), which is why we ban PR chasing
- Same-day training: lift first if forced, ≥6h separation preserves gains (Robineau 2016)
- Modality preference: cycling / rowing / ski-erg > running for a strength athlete (Wilson 2012)
- Protein floor: 1.6 g/kg/day (Morton 2018 meta)
