## Test 3 · hanging_leg_raise
URL: https://youtu.be/Pr1ieGZ5atk
Channel: ATHLEAN-X (Jeff Cavaliere, MSPT, CSCS)
Length: ~4 minutes (longer than ideal but the format is a coach walking through the movement, no brand intro)
Why: Trusted channel on your allow-list; Cavaliere is a licensed physical therapist; the video explicitly covers the raise and lowering phase mechanics on a bar with clear side-view. No dedicated "click in lowering phase" video exists on trusted channels — this is the best available for the mechanics that produce that click. Users watch to understand the movement; they self-observe the click themselves.
