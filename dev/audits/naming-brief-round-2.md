# Naming — Round 2 shared brief

## Where round 1 landed

Three agents ran on the general "English name for the app" brief. Convergence:
**Hone** in every top 3; two of three agents made it their cheque pick. But
the founder's preferred direction is **Sharpen** — direct English of the
current name (Terav = "sharp" in Estonian), zero explainer, every piece of
copy already uses the verb.

## What round 2 is testing

Two things:

1. **Is there a name in the Sharpen-family that's better than Sharpen itself?**
   The founder likes Sharpen for its transparency but is open to a
   sharper (excuse the pun) alternative from the same semantic family:
   words that describe refining, tuning, dialling in, cleaning up,
   focusing. Adjacent verbs, adjacent nouns, adjacent metaphors.

2. **Are there CrossFit / fitness-community-native words or phrases the
   audience already uses that we've missed?** The target user talks a
   specific vocabulary — "dial it in," "lock in," "level up," "sharpen
   up," "prime the pump," "engine," "capacity," "threshold," "PR,"
   "AMRAP." A brand that already speaks this vocabulary lands faster
   than one that has to teach the word.

## Product recap

- Adaptive training app; every session sharpens the plan
- Programs by domain (aerobic, concurrent, skill, rehab)
- Cited studies as backbone
- Confirm-first adaptive engine (no silent changes)
- Personalisation on your weakness — the app finds and works your gap

## Audience recap

- CrossFitters, strength athletes, rehab-adjacent lifters
- 25-45 years old, English-first, international
- Reads sport-science newsletters, values honesty and evidence
- Suspicious of "wellness" or "startup-tech" names
- Uses actual gym-floor vocabulary

## Constraints (from round 1, still valid)

- Survives as a URL (.com hard, .fit / .app soft)
- Survives as a monogram (T on chisel-stroke is current, can change)
- Survives as a verb in a CTA
- Survives on the box wall — "I'm on ___ this cycle"
- No overlap with existing fitness brands (Whoop, Strava, Peloton, Zwift,
  Fitbod, Freeletics, Runna, Tempo — check your candidates)
- No pronunciation ambiguity, no medical connotation, no AI/tech vibe

## Deliverable per agent

1. **12-18 candidates**, each with:
   - Name
   - Origin / where it comes from (CrossFit slang, English verb, invented)
   - Why it fits (2 sentences)
   - Why it might not (1 sentence, honest)
   - CTA test: "___ the plan tonight." — reads how?
   - Trademark / domain read (if obviously blocked, note it)

2. **Head-to-head vs Sharpen**: for each candidate, one sentence on
   whether it beats or loses to Sharpen and why.

3. **Shortlist of your top 3** with paragraph.

4. **Cheque pick** — one paragraph.
