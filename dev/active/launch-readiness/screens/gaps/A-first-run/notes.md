First Strict Pull-Up

From no hang to a rep you own.

WHAT IT'S FOR
You want a strict pull-up — not a kipping one. That's a different skill.
You can't dead-hang 15 seconds yet, or you get 1-2 and want 5.
You'd rather train the weakest link than just do more pull-ups.
You have a bar and a band, and 15-30 minutes, three or four times a week.
THE ARC

Four tiers — Hang, Assisted, First Rep, Volume. Intake places you, then each week targets whichever sub-capability is furthest behind: grip, scapular control, row strength, or negative control. A beginner spends weeks on hangs and scap pulls before touching a full rep; a first-repper goes straight to volume work.

WHAT GETS RETESTED

Hang time retested weekly at the lower tiers, max reps weekly at the upper. Full assessment every 4 weeks — dead hang, scap pull, ring row, negative control.

WHAT EACH STAGE LOOKS LIKE
HANG
A steady dead hang, clean scap pulls and your first ring rows.
ASSISTED
Controlled negatives and band-assisted reps, building toward a first strict rep.
FIRST REP
Your first strict reps, with the pattern grooved.
VOLUME
Sets of unbroken reps, with grip variations unlocked.

Not guarantees. People respond to training differently.

SECTION 1 OF 4

Who this is for

You want a strict pull-up. Some athletes can't dead-hang 15 seconds yet; some do 1-2 reps and want to hit 5. The app meets you where you are and builds each sub-capability separately. Not for kipping pull-ups — that's a different skill.

SECTION 2 OF 4

What you'll achieve

Beginners: 25-45 second dead hang, clean scap pulls, first ring rows. Assisted-only lifters: first strict rep from a 10-second negative. First-repper: 3-5 unbroken. Volume tier: 8-10 unbroken with wide-grip variety unlocked.

SECTION 3 OF 4

What it takes
8 weeks · ~1.5 hr/week (3-4 sessions/wk, 15-30 min each)
Levels · Hang → Assisted → First Rep → Volume

ADAPTS TO YOU

Intake picks your tier; drills adapt weekly to your weakest capability (grip, scap, row, negative). Every session sharpens further from your logs.

SECTION 4 OF 4

How we prove it works

Weekly max-rep retest (Tier C/D) or weekly hang-time retest (Tier A/B); every 4 weeks: full assessment (dead hang, scap pull, ring row, strict pull-up, negative control).

Where you start

First Strict Pull-Up

RECOMMENDED

Tier D — Multiple reps, adding volume

By week 8: 8-10 strict pull-ups unbroken from a starting 3-5 rep max. Wide-grip and neutral-grip variants unlocked. Weighted pull-up prep starts at end of block.

EXPLAIN-BACK

How we picked this

We used: your intake answers plus your physical test results (dead hang max seconds ≈ 3 seconds, scap pull max reps ≈ 3 reps, ring row max reps feet elevated ≈ 3 reps).

Physical tests carry the most weight because they're measured numbers. Intake answers confirm the direction. Confident pick.

DON'T AGREE? PICK A DIFFERENT STARTING POINT

Tier A — No hang yet

By week 8: 25-45 second dead hang, first clean scapular pulls, and 5-10 strict feet-elevated ring rows.

Tier B — Hang established, no strict rep yet

By week 8-10: first strict pull-up OR a clean 10-second slow negative + a heavy-band assisted rep at low band tension. Dead hang at 45-60 seconds.

Tier C — One strict rep, building the second

By week 8: 3-5 strict pull-ups unbroken. Grease-the-groove daily singles unlock. First tempo pull-ups (3s up, 3s hold, 3s down).

Tier D — Multiple reps, adding volume

By week 8: 8-10 strict pull-ups unbroken from a starting 3-5 rep max. Wide-grip and neutral-grip variants unlocked. Weighted pull-up prep starts at end of block.
