# GMB Fitness (gmb.io) competitor scan, accessed 2026-10-27

## Facts
- Format: one-time purchase programmes, "pay once and keep it forever", updates included. Memberships (Alpha Posse, All-In) exist, billing not stated. https://get.gmb.io/faq (accessed 2026-10-27)
- Prices (conflicting sources): FAQ says Elements "is $150", Mobility "$95" (https://get.gmb.io/faq). Elements page snippet says Basic $95 or 3x$55, and "Elements & Mobility" $195 (https://gmb.io/elements, search snippet only; page returned HTTP 403 to fetch). Autonomy Plus $995 listed (https://gmb.io/programs/ search snippet). Integral Strength and Resilience: no price on FAQ page.
- Terms: 90-day "moving noticeably better" refund on standalone programmes, not memberships; about 3% of clients ask for refunds (https://get.gmb.io/faq). A 30-day trial is mentioned in a search snippet only (https://reviews.io/company-reviews/store/gmb.io).
- Promise and audience: "Over 125,000 clients" since 2010 (https://get.gmb.io/faq); "83,000 clients" for Elements (https://gmb.io/elements search snippet). Claims 15-45 minutes, a few times a week. Self-reported, not independent.
- Reviews: Trustpilot 3.5-3.6 from 5 reviews (https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/gmb.io, accessed 2026-10-27). Complaints: uneven progressions, no session index, mandatory post-session quiz, rigid refunds (same page, Feb 2026 and Aug 2023 reviews). Reviews.io shows 4.9 from 571 reviews (https://reviews.io/company-reviews/store/gmb.io), which may be curated. Search snippet says some find Elements "too basic" (Reddit thread, not fetched).
- Acquisition: free YouTube tutorials plus reviews on Reddit, YouTube and Facebook (https://get.gmb.io/faq). Ryan Hurst is a GMB co-founder (https://www.wholelifechallenge.com/80-ryan-hurst-how-to-exercise-with-purpose-and-playfulness/).
- YouTube size: Gymnastic Bodies channel 42,161 subs per socialcounts.org, undated; about 20.3K per FeedSpot 2026 list (https://videos.feedspot.com/gymnastics_youtube_channels/). Link to GMB is an inference.
- Integral Strength: no pricing, subscriber count or sales page found. Not found.
- Handstand comparables (one-time, 2026 snippets): Barbell Physio Handstand Balance Breakthrough $49.99 or $34.99/mo (https://thebarbellphysio.com/portfolio_page/handstand-balance-breakthrough/); Kononov Club $9 launch price (https://www.kononov.club/handstand). Prices are launch or vendor-stated, not verified.

## What it means for Terav
- Threat: GMB sells a one-time, lifetime-access curriculum at $95-150 with a 90-day refund and 125k claimed clients. Terav's EUR 9.99/mo subscription is a higher ongoing cost against a cheaper, owned product.
- Gap: GMB's complaint list (no session index, rigid progression, quiz friction) is close to what Terav's log-driven adaptation addresses. Terav's log-to-proposal loop is not something a fixed course offers.
- Gap: GMB offers no adaptation to logs and no per-focus mechanic. Its refund and lifetime-access model is a cost-of-entry reference point for the 14-day trial.
- Lesson: Price anchor of about EUR 90-150 one-off is the established reference. Consider an annual plan near EUR 89 framed against it.
- Lesson: Trust signals come from YouTube free content and Reddit. Terav's paid-acquisition plan does not replace that content-led path.

## Verdict signal
suggests-add:a one-off or annual price option and a visible refund term, to match the GMB anchor. Not a pivot signal.

## Confidence
Low-medium. Price conflicts between sources (FAQ vs. snippet), the Elements page returned 403, audience and subscriber numbers are self-reported or undated, Integral Strength not found, and only 5 Trustpilot reviews.
