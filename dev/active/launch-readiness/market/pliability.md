## What it means for Terav
- Threat: Pliability proves consumers pay about $20/mo for mobility, and Terav at ~€9.99 is priced below it. Pliability's breadth and 9K-review base compete for the same "mobility" search intent.
- Gap: Pliability is generalist and athlete-skewed; no single-goal, log-adaptive or per-change citation model appears in any retrieved source.
- Gap: Its complaints centre on paywall friction and trial confusion. Terav should state trial terms identically everywhere.
- Lesson: Its homepage offers unsourced stats ("Our Data says", "1" for lower mortality). Terav's cited-change approach is a visible differentiator, but only if citations are shown in-product.
- Lesson: Pliability's HYROX partnership shows event-tied focus works. Terav's skill/engine programs can use the same logic.
