- Adapty: hard paywall 10.7% download-to-paid by day 35 vs freemium 2.1% (unnamed 2026 industry figure); one client CPI $3-5 rising to $8 (no region). https://adapty.io/blog/meta-ads-subscription-apps/

## What it means for Terav
- Threat: H&F has high LTV per payer but rising CPM (+38% health and wellness), so paid cost per trial is likely to rise, not fall.
- Gap: no retrieved source gives cost per trial start for fitness; the EUR 100-200 test cannot be sized from benchmarks.
- Sample size (my calculation, not sourced): 25% vs 40% trial-to-paid needs ~150 trials per arm at 80% power. A EUR 150 test at an assumed EUR 5-8 per trial buys ~20-30 trials, enough to see trial starts, not conversion.
- Learning: a EUR 150 test is below the ~50 events/week learning threshold, so it can measure click-to-trial intent, not payback.
- Lesson: 14-day trial is within the range RevenueCat links to better conversion (all-app 17-32 day trials 45.7%, not H&F-specific).
