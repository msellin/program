# Fitness app market size, 2024-2026 (accessed 2026-10-27)

## Facts
- Sensor Tower, 2024 data: global Health & Fitness downloads 3.6B, +6% YoY; US >50% of global IAP revenue, UK 8%. https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-mobile-health-and-fitness-in-2025 (fetched)
- Sensor Tower, Jan 2025: H&F IAP $385M, +10% YoY, all-time monthly high; the same page projects >$4B global H&F IAP for 2025 (a forecast, not a result). Same URL.
- Secondary, unverified against primary: 2025 H&F IAP ~$4.5B (+13%), installs 3.96B (+0.8%). Source is a low-quality aggregator citing Sensor Tower, not the report itself. https://voxbooster.com/blog/fitness-app-statistics-2026 (search snippet only)
- RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026, H&F category: median trial-to-paid 37.7% (top quartile >51.4%); median D14 revenue per install $0.48, D60 $0.66; median month-1 RLTV per payer $24.23, Y1 $35.64, top performers >$60. Page contradicts itself on annual-plan share (68% vs 59%). https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-business (fetched)
- Adapty 2026: H&F trial-to-paid 35.0%; annual plans 60.6% of H&F subs. https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions/ (search snippet only, not fetched)
- Business of Apps: 2025 fitness app store revenue $3.4B, +24.5%. Secondhand via the aggregator above; the BoA page returned HTTP 403. Not verified.
- Concentration (revenue, category level): not found. Sweatcoin held ~a quarter of MAU share among fitness-tracking apps in 2024 (Sensor Tower, MAU not revenue).

## What it means for Terav
- Threat: the category is large and still growing in spend, so consumers pay. Expect the same category ad inventory to be contested by many subscription apps.
- Gap: no category-level data separates focused single-goal coaching from general training apps. Market size cannot tell whether Terav's niche has demand.
- Lesson: 14-day RPI median $0.48 means a Meta/Google install must earn about half a euro of revenue within two weeks before it breaks even on revenue, before any margin. Check CAC against that.
- Lesson: a 14-day trial aligns with the median trial-to-paid (~38%). Realistic target: 38% median, 51% top quartile.
- Lesson: annual plan prominence is supported (both sources show annual-heavy H&F), so the €89/yr option is consistent with the category.

## Verdict signal
no-signal (category totals support that paying fitness users exist; they say nothing about focused-goal demand)

## Confidence
Med on category direction and conversion benchmarks (two primary sources, one fetched in full, the other partially). Low on 2025 totals (aggregator-only), concentration (not found), and any niche demand claim.
