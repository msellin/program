# Pricing and trial benchmarks (Health & Fitness), accessed 2026-10-27

## Facts
- H&F median monthly/annual price: not found in any primary source retrieved. Vendor band (USD, US storefront, no sample size): mid-market $9.99-14.99/mo, $59.99-89.99/yr; author suggests annual at ~6-7x monthly. https://appsops.store/blog/health-fitness-app-subscription-pricing-2026 (Aug 2026)
- Annual share of H&F revenue rose 51% to 61% (2023-2025); only App Store category where annual keeps gaining. https://adapty.io/blog/health-fitness-app-subscription-benchmarks/ (Mar 27 2026)
- Install-to-trial 9.5%, trial-to-paid 42.2%, first renewal 67.7% for H&F weekly plans with trial (year not stated). Same source.
- Trial-to-paid median by trial length, all categories 2026: 17-32 day trials 42.5%; under 4 days 25.5%. 3/7/14-day values not in text (chart only). https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/7-day-trial-subscription-app.md and https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026
- Trials of 4 days or less: 42.1% (2025) to 46.5% (2026) of trials. H&F, Education, Travel commonly use 5-9 day trials. Same RevenueCat sources.
- H&F median trial-to-paid 39.9% (2025 report, earlier methodology; not comparable to 2026). https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025 (accessed via search snippet; not fetched)
- Hard paywall Day-35 median 10.7% (2026); freemium 2.1%. RevenueCat subscription-app-trends page above.
- 14-day vs 7-day head-to-head: no numbers retrieved. Headspace 14/7 split reported "strong conversion lift", no figure (RevenueCat trends page).
- Card-required vs no-card trials: not found. Soft vs hard paywall for H&F: not found.
- Web checkout vs app-store pricing: not found.

## What it means for Terav
- Gap: no primary H&F dataset on 14-day trials; the 14-day recommendation rests on an unverified Phiture citation.
- Threat: 14 days is outside the 5-9 day norm RevenueCat describes for H&F; a longer trial delays first cash and is untested against Terav's own data.
- Lesson: pricing band fits; annual at EUR 89 is about 8.9x monthly, above the 6-7x guidance, so annual is the lever to watch.
- Lesson: track day-0 and 90-day retention, not trial-start counts (vendor caveat, not a primary finding).
- Lesson: the Terav mechanism (confirm-first proposals) is not represented in any retrieved benchmark; no evidence either way.

## Verdict signal
supports-as-built (EUR 9.99/mo sits inside the mid-market band; EUR 89/yr is at the top edge; 14-day trial length is unresolved, no-signal on that axis)

## Confidence
Low. Price bands come from a single vendor blog with no sample size; the 3/7/14-day conversion split is only in RevenueCat chart images; no card-required or web-checkout data retrieved. Figures from 2025 and 2026 reports are not directly comparable.
