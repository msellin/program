## What it means for Terav
- Threat: GOWOD is free-to-start, adaptive (assessment plus retests) and cheap, with strong store ratings and a CrossFit partnership. Direct overlap with Terav's "adapts to your log" pitch for mobility and CrossFit users.
- Threat: Terav's skill and strength focus does not overlap GOWOD's mobility niche, so the overlap is mainly with the overhead-mobility program.
- Gap: GOWOD complaints (repetitive routines, no equipment or length control, crashes) point to a demand for tailored, controllable sessions, which Terav's confirm-first model addresses.
- Gap: no GOWOD evidence citations found. Terav's "each change cites a study" is a differentiator to test in copy.
- Lesson: price anchors are $90-145/yr. Terav's planned €89/yr sits at the low end, so the price is not a barrier to entry but is not a premium signal.

## Verdict signal
no-signal (GOWOD's niche is mobility with strong incumbency; it says little about demand for single-focus strength or skill programs.)
