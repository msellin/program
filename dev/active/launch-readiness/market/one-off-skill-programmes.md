## What it means for Terav
- Threat: one-off handstand and pull-up programmes are priced $4-$299 and sell with lifetime access and money-back promises, so a sub-€10/month price competes with near-zero entry cost.
- Gap: no retrieved seller offers an adaptive, log-driven version; the one subscription found (Hybrid Calisthenics) is general, not goal-specific.
- Gap: sellers sell "results", not ongoing adjustment, so a recurring-value claim is untested by this evidence.
- Lesson: a 14-day trial at ~EUR 10/month is priced inside the range of single-purchase guides; the pitch must rest on the adaptation, not the content.
- Lesson: money-back guarantees are common here; Terav's trial is the equivalent and should be stated plainly.
