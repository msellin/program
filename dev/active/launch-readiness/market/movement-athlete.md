## What it means for Terav
- Threat: TMA already sells the exact "assess, place, progress skill" promise at a lower entry price (USD 24.99/mo, store listing) with a 7-day trial, and a 100+ move library. Terav's skill-goal pitch overlaps directly.
- Gap: TMA's placement covers nine fundamentals and general progression, not a single-goal arc tied to a log. No evidence it cites studies or shows why a change was proposed.
- Gap: user complaints on support latency and bugs suggest reliability and support are weak points Terav can beat.
- Lesson: keep the "steps with many small progressions" framing; TMA's praise is for granularity, not breadth.
- Lesson: outcome copy that names the real starting point and injury limits ("works around injuries") is the market's sought-after message.
