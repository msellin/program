# Competitor: Fitbod (fitbod.me), accessed 2026-10-27

## Facts
- Homepage promise "LESS PLANNING. MORE PROGRESS."; algorithm "tracks your logged workout data to dynamically personalize every workout"; claims all fitness levels, with a stated following among intermediate/advanced lifters. No prices or trial terms on homepage. https://www.fitbod.me (accessed 2026-10-27)
- Price $15.99/mo or $95.99/yr for new subscribers (up from $12.99 / $79.99); legacy subscribers keep old price until cancel and resubscribe. 7-day time-based trial, "no permanent free tier", trial rolls into paid unless cancelled. Third-party review, not Fitbod's own FAQ. https://www.sensai.fit/blog/fitbod-review-2026 (accessed 2026-10-27)
- Price conflict: a pricing aggregator still anchors on $12.99/mo. https://subger.com/en/service/fitbod (accessed 2026-10-27, via search only, not fetched)
- Trustpilot: TrustScore 3.0/5 from 38 reviews. Top complaints: billing/cancellation/refunds (trials converting to charges), workout repetitiveness and crashes, slow support. https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.fitbod.me (accessed 2026-10-27)
- Paid social: third-party ad trackers show Fitbod Meta ads; one Jan-2024 ad ~501K views, est. spend ~$6.5K (AdScan estimate). https://adscan.ai/ad/898747648460133 (accessed 2026-10-27, search snippet only). A Motion summary of a 2026 ad cites "Try Fitbod free" and "15 million downloads / 120 million logged workouts" (vendor-reported, unverified). https://library.motionapp.com/fitbod (accessed 2026-10-27, search snippet only)
- TV spots "Personalized", "Being Better", "Results" listed on iSpot. https://www.ispot.tv/ad/bMdc/fitbod-personalized (accessed 2026-10-27, search snippet only)
- Award: "Best AI Workout App of 2026" (Garage Gym Reviews), cited in SensAI review; not fetched. Not found: official user counts, revenue, app-store rating.
- Reviewer criticism (SensAI, a competitor): prescribes without explaining reasoning; no HRV/sleep inputs; recommendations improve only "after a few weeks"; plateaus handled by lowering weight or swapping exercises.

## What it means for Terav
- Threat: Fitbod already sells "adaptive, algorithm-personalised" at $15.99/mo with a 7-day trial and paid acquisition. Terav at ~EUR 9.99 undercuts it, but the category is crowded on price.
- Gap: Fitbod's complaints are billing, crashes, and "it prescribes, doesn't explain". Terav's cited-study, Accept/Ignore model answers the explanation complaint directly.
- Gap: Fitbod is generic strength; Terav's single-goal focus (pull-up, handstand, rehab) is not what Fitbod sells.
- Lesson: Fitbod's wording is "less planning, more progress" (outcome, no mechanism). For non-experts, lead with the outcome and show the mechanism only on tap.
- Lesson: Trial-to-charge disputes are the top review theme. A clear pre-charge reminder and easy cancel reduces chargebacks and bad reviews.
