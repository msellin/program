# Rowing / erg / engine apps: market signal (access date 2026-10-27)

## Facts
- ErgZone: free app; ErgZone+ in-app purchases EZ+ Monthly $4.99, EZ+ Annual $49.99; training plans from coaches; "Organize Your Training" listed under the paid tier. 501 ratings, 4.8. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1474566270
- ErgData (Concept2): free workout tracker for all three Concept2 ergs; forum consensus pairs it with a free plan. https://www.c2forum.com/viewtopic.php?p=469147 (thread dated Apr 2019)
- Free plans: Pete Plan (3-week cycle) and Wolverine Plan (long, low-rate sessions, criticised for complexity). Versions and current availability not verified. https://c2forum.com/viewtopic.php?p=314975 ; https://brokenoarspodcast.podbean.com/e/broken-oars-great-training-plans-of-our-time-the-wolverine-plan-vs-the-pete-plan
- Splash Rowing (Concept2 and race training): free, "no subscription", adaptive to total fatigue and goals, auto-syncs the Concept2 Logbook; no 2k-specific workout named; no ratings yet; v1.3.3 released Jun 13 (year not shown). https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splash-rowing/id6756917153
- Row Forge (PM5 only): subscription with ability-scaled targets and retests; price not found. https://apps.apple.com/bb/app/row-forge/id1449786284
- Rowing Log - Erg: 2K zone table and predictor; pro tier $39.99/yr (search snippet, not fetched). https://appshunter.io/ios/app/rowing-log-erg/id6774305705
- 2k coach plans sold as TrainingPeaks products; one 2k plan is 8 weeks with coach reviews; price not found. https://www.trainingpeaks.com/training-plans/other/rowing/tp-221562/2k-advanced-training
- Hydrow membership about $44-50/month per 2026 review sites (search snippets; official page fetch rate-limited). https://hydrow.com/en-GB/blog/hydrow-membership-cost-pricing-guide-faqs/
- Zone 2 apps: many free (Zone2 Pulse, OwnPace); paid tiers $9.99/yr (Zone2 Fitness) to $49.99 lifetime. https://apps.apple.com/app/id6444166065 (snippet-level)
- "The Engine" by Jon Horn: not found.
- Concept2 RowErg hardware about $990 with no subscription (snippet). Not verified on Concept2 site.

## What it means for Terav
- Threat: free ErgData, Pete/Wolverine plans and free Splash Rowing (adaptive, Concept2 sync) cover the "what do I row this week" job at zero cost.
- Threat: ErgZone+ at $49.99/yr sets an observed annual ceiling for erg-app subscriptions, well under the planned EUR 89/yr.
- Gap: no adaptive 2k test-prep plan with cited rationale surfaced in the results; Splash does not name 2k.
- Gap: Terav's confirm-first and citation layer is not visible in any competitor listing found.
- Lesson: the free competitors win on import (Concept2 Logbook sync); Terav needs logbook import to avoid a manual-entry penalty.
