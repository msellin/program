# Retention and churn: subscription fitness apps (accessed 2026-10-27)

## Facts
- Health & Fitness first-renewal retention 30.3%, lowest of any category; trial-to-paid 35.0%, highest; annual plans 60.6% of revenue. Monthly/annual churn and 12-month retention not found on the page. https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions/
- RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026 (page summary, not the full 339-page report): H&F trial-to-paid median 37.7%; day-35 download-to-paid 2.9%; yearly LTV after Y1 $35.64. Annual share conflicts inside the page (68% vs 59%). Refund and H&F churn not found. Day-0 cancellation of 3-day trials 55.4%; AI apps churn ~30% faster. https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps
- Fitness monthly voluntary churn 8-12% (2026). Search snippet only, not fetched, unverified. https://mwm.ai/glossary/churn
- Goal-achievement: 115M logged activities, >1M users, 31 months, mobile activity app. Users are more likely to stop once their primary goal is reached, and many return when the original intent resurfaces. Share who stop not found. https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.08972
- Goal difficulty (An, Bonfrer, Eckert, JMR, reported by UTS, Mar 2026): moderately challenging weekly targets predict activity one year later; very easy or very hard targets less effective. Investment app, not fitness; no retention %. https://www.uts.edu.au/news/2026/03/goal-setting-apps-can-backfire-if-goals-are-too-easy--or-too-hard
- Runna: no post-goal or post-plan churn data found. Plan Replay end-of-plan recap exists (product description, not fetched). RevenueCat case study reports calendar-connected users retain better (snippet only). https://www-docs.revenuecat.com/customers/runna
- Not found: Couch-to-5K retention; skill-app (handstand, pull-up) post-goal retention; fitness refund rates.

## What it means for Terav
- Threat: H&F has the weakest renewal (30%) despite the best trial conversion, so a 14-day trial at EUR 9.99/month will convert but may not renew. Annual (EUR 89) fits the category norm.
- Gap: Terav's "one focus" model is exactly the goal-completion case where the 2018 study finds users stop. No evidence found that a next-goal or block handoff retains them.
- Lesson: Moderate goal difficulty helps retention (UTS, non-fitness). Terav's progression gate should be treated as a retention lever, not only a safety feature.
- Lesson: Plan a post-goal path (next focus, maintenance track) before the first pull-up goal date arrives, not after churn.
- Lesson: Measure involuntary billing churn (Google Play cancellations ~1/3 involuntary per RevenueCat) from day one.

## Verdict signal

## Confidence
Low-medium. Category figures come from Adapty and RevenueCat summaries; no fitness goal-app retention cohort was found; the goal-stop study is a 2018 activity tracker, not a goal-coaching app.
