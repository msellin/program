## What it means for Terav
- Threat: HWPO's $40/mo band and 14-day trial match Terav's planned €9.99 plus 14-day trial. Terav's price is lower, but the sell is narrower (one goal).
- Gap: The "one focus alongside your week" pitch is not visibly sold by the big CrossFit brands. The closest is PRVN Burn/skills, bundled at no separate price, and not evidenced as a standalone product.
- Lesson: Marketplace coaches sell single-focus plans at $10-$39/mo, so a single-focus product at under €10 sits in an existing price band.
- Lesson: Trial-first pricing (14 days) is the category norm, not a differentiator.
- Lesson: Sources are weak. Verify PRVN and Mayhem prices before quoting them.
