# Runna (runna.com) market check, accessed 2026-10-27

## Facts
- Pricing (runna.com/pricing, accessed 2026-10-27): monthly EUR 19.99, annual EUR 119.99 (EUR 9.99/mo, 50% saving), USD 19.99 / 119.99. Both show a free 7-day trial, cancel anytime. Headline: "Unlock your potential with a personalized plan." https://runna.com/pricing
- Strava + Runna bundle: USD 149.99/yr, annual only, billed by Strava; 30-day trial for new Strava users. Trial lengths by route: 7 days direct, 14 days in-Strava-app for first-time users, 2 weeks via referral code. Source: https://stack-archive.com/blog/is-runna-free-price-trial-strava-bundle-2026/ (article dated 2026-10-16 per page)
- Free tier: only the first week of any plan is free. Free beginner plans reportedly ended 5 June 2026 (third-party claim, unverified). https://the5krunner.com/2026/10/14/is-runna-free/
- Acquisition: Strava announced acquisition of Runna 17 April 2025; price undisclosed. Runna founded 2021, London, ~150 staff. Strava had ~150M registered users. Apps stay separate for now; Runna already uses Strava's API. Source: https://www.advnture.com/tech/apps-software/a-perfect-fit-stravas-150-million-users-get-a-new-running-coach-as-brand-acquires-coaching-app-runna (article dated 2025-04-23)
- Ratings: advnture (2025) cites 5-star average on Apple App Store from 16,000+ reviews (not re-verified). Trustpilot UK TrustScore 4/5 from ~1K reviews (search snippet only, page not fetched). https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/runna.com
- Complaints (search summaries, not fully fetched): Apple Watch app unreliable, runs lost; subscription/cancellation friction; customer service dissatisfaction. Praise: coaching, plans, UI. Sources: https://kimola.com/reports/unlock-insights-with-our-runna-app-customer-feedback-report-app-store-us-156409 and https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1594204443/runna-lauftrainer-coach/reviews
- Not found: user count, revenue, ad spend, influencer strategy, and whether Runna's plans are "adaptive" in Terav's sense (it is marketed as "personalized plan").

## What it means for Terav
- Threat: Strava's distribution (150M registered users) plus bundle pricing undercuts a standalone running coach; Terav's engine-builder/rowing-2k overlap is in running-adjacent endurance.
- Gap: Runna sells one sport (running) with a race-goal promise; no evidence of a single-goal skill or rehab-adjacent product. Terav's skill/rehab categories are not contested by this source.
- Lesson: a single-goal plan app has a proven paid market at ~EUR 10/mo effective annual, so the price anchor for Terav's EUR 9.99 is in range.
- Lesson: trial mechanics (7-day direct vs 14-day referral) show the category competes on trial length; Terav's 14-day trial is at the top of that range.
- Lesson: recurring complaints are device sync and cancellation friction; both are cheap to get right and visible in reviews.

## Verdict signal
no-signal (Runna evidences a paid single-sport plan market; it does not test Terav's multi-goal, confirm-first model)

## Confidence
Medium on pricing (official page fetched) and acquisition (two sources). Low on ratings, complaints and user counts (search snippets and third-party aggregators only; App Store figures not verified).
