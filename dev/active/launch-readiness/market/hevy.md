# Hevy (hevyapp.com) competitor scan, accessed 2026-10-27

## Facts
- Free: unlimited logging; capped at 4 routines, 7 custom exercises, 3 months of graph and measurement history. Pro: $2.99/mo, $23.99/yr, or $74.99 lifetime; a $3.99 monthly SKU also appears. Sources: https://www.hevyapp.com/compare/symmetry/ (Hevy's own page); https://www.sensai.fit/blog/hevy-review-2026 (third party).
- Hevy help center (https://help.hevyapp.com/hc/en-us/articles/33106320824727-Everything-You-Need-to-Know-About-the-Hevy-App-2026-Features-Guide) returned HTTP 403 to my fetch; not verified there.
- Hevy Trainer (Pro only; launched 18 Feb 2026 per https://prpath.app/blog/hevy-app-review-2026.html) is labelled by Hevy an "adaptive strength programming system based on a sophisticated algorithm", not AI. Inputs: experience, goal, equipment, frequency, duration, muscle focus. Progression: add weight once the top of the rep range is hit on all sets. No sleep, HRV, or readiness input documented. Source: https://www.sensai.fit/blog/hevy-review-2026.
- Hevy has no native AI coach. A ChatGPT integration can generate or update routines (free up to 4 routines). Source: https://www.hevyapp.com/compare/symmetry/.
- Size: Hevy claims 16-17M athletes. Store ratings conflict: 4.9 on 360k+ App Store ratings (Hevy page) vs 4.9 on about 92k US App Store ratings (sensai). Store listings not fetched directly.
- Growth channel (organic, social, ads): not found.
- Complaints (secondary, weakly sourced, search snippets): barbell weight must be logged as total; no permanent notes; no recovery inputs; no Garmin integration (Garmin rejected API access); occasional sync issues. Sources: https://www.sensai.fit/blog/hevy-review-2026, https://repreturn.com/hevy-app-review/. Capterra/G2 complaints concern Hevy Coach (trainer product), not this consumer app.

## What it means for Terav
- Threat: Trainer is a cheap (~$3/mo), algorithmic program generator with weight progression inside a huge install base. Terav's adaptation must be visibly more than fixed-rule progression.
- Gap: no documented recovery or readiness input in Hevy Trainer; Terav's symptom gate is a differentiator only if it is live and visible.
- Gap: no focused single-goal mode or cited, confirm-first change proposals documented for Hevy.
- Lesson: Terav's ~EUR 9.99/mo is about 3x Hevy Pro. Price must be justified by the proposal-and-citation mechanic, not by logging features.
- Lesson: Hevy caps free routines and history rather than features; a 14-day trial is a difference (sensai says Hevy Pro has no free trial, unverified).

## Verdict signal
suggests-add: a recovery or readiness input feeding the adaptation loop, visible to the user (Hevy Trainer documents none).

## Confidence
Low-medium. Prices agree across Hevy's page and one blog (medium). Size figures conflict and store listings were not checked (low). Growth channels not found. Complaints rest on blogs with no primary source (low). Help center blocked (403).
