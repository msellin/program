## What it means for Terav
- Threat: AI workout generation is cheap and commoditised (Fitbod about $13-16/mo, ChatGPT free). Terav's price (~EUR 9.99) sits in that band, so price alone is no moat.
- Threat: Future reversed its AI product within months. Evidence that a free-form AI coach is not yet a retained product, though the reason was not disclosed.
- Gap: no competitor surveyed cites studies per adjustment or asks the user to confirm each change. Fitbod and Ladder both sell without that. This is Terav's distinct position.
- Gap: Terav's single-goal framing is not claimed by these apps, which are full-plan products.
- Lesson: keep the confirm-first and cited-change mechanism as the headline. Don't position as "AI coach", which the market treats as a commodity.

## Verdict signal
supports-as-built (AI-coach category is crowded, so positioning and citations must carry it; no evidence for a pivot)

## Confidence
Low-medium. Prices come mostly from affiliate and competitor blogs that contradict each other; only Ladder's and Future's own pages were partly corroborated. No demand or retention data for any AI coach was retrieved, so the verdict is inferred from pricing and one reversal.
