# Competitor: Ladder (joinladder.com), accessed 2026-10-27

## Facts
- Pricing: Pro Monthly $29.99/mo; Pro Annual $179.99/yr ("$14.99/mo"). Trial 7 days, no card, "No payment info is collected until AFTER your trial ends." https://joinladder.com/pricing (2026-10-27)
- Pro includes 1 team, 2 team changes/month; Annual adds all teams + unlimited changes. Coaching = in-ear audio + video demos + Team Chat tips. https://joinladder.com/pricing
- Promise: "Members follow complete, coach-designed weekly programs"; "built for busy people who are already motivated to train." https://www.joinladder.com (2026-10-27)
- Explicitly "not algorithm-generated, not 1-on-1 remote personal training, and not class-led"; a human coach designs a new 7-day plan weekly per team. https://www.joinladder.com
