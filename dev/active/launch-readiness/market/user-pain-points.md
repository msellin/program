# User pain points: single-goal training (access date 2026-10-27)

## Facts
- Plateau on first strict pull-up is a common complaint; a coaching guide names "trying a pull-up every session" as a stall cause and advises testing every 2 weeks. "Failing reps every session just builds frustration and bad form." https://www.findyouredge.app/news/how-to-do-your-first-pull-up-beginners-guide (fetched; no date shown)
- Community anecdote: "stuck at 10 reps for about four months" and advice that a long plateau suggests form problems. https://twt.omada.cafe/r/bodyweightfitness/comments/1nk46v8/managed_5_pullups_in_a_row_yesterday/newtd2r/?context=3 (search snippet only, mirror site, not fetched)
- Skill-add advice: keep gymnastics volume low, "2 to 3 gymnastics exercises" is reasonable; handstand can be frequent if volume is controlled. https://www.strongfirst.com/community/goto/post?id=486273 (search snippet only)
- Concurrent training: review says strength-only groups gained more max strength; interference depends on protocol. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21425890/ (search snippet only; PubMed page failed to load, abstract not verified)
- British Rowing advises strength after endurance or 8+ hours apart. https://britishrowing.org/?p=46534 (search snippet only; fetch returned 403)
- AI plan distrust: Runna injury concern; "no peer-reviewed study has yet established" higher injury rates. https://www.techradar.com/health-fitness/are-ai-training-apps-like-runna-putting-you-at-risk-of-injury-i-asked-a-real-life-running-coach (search snippet; article body truncated on fetch, so the quote is unverified)
- Hevy: 4.9/5 from 65.3K reviews; no specific distrust of its AI plans found. https://marlvel.ai/intel-report/health-fitness/com-hevyapp-hevy (third-party aggregator, unverified)
- Competing single-skill apps exist with progression paths and "tailored" claims (Fitloop, Handstand Quest, Golden Pull Ups); these are developer listings. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1474941254 ; https://apps.apple.com/ci/app/handstand-quest/id1482090288
- Not found: any Reddit or App Store thread complaining about generic single-goal programmes; any 2026 review of AI plan trust in Hevy.

## What it means for Terav
- Threat: single-skill apps (Fitloop, Handstand Quest, Golden Pull Ups) already occupy the pull-up and handstand niches with free or cheap tracking.
- Gap: the plateau pain is about form and testing frequency; Terav's log-driven adjustment addresses this only if it surfaces form/test-cadence signals, which is not evidenced here.
- Gap: interference (rowing 2k plus strength) is a real planning problem; the sources support sequencing, which Terav's "runs alongside your week" framing must actually do.
- Lesson: the cited-study, confirm-first design answers the trust pain seen with AI plans, but no retrieved source shows users asking for it.
- Lesson: keep the claim narrow; do not promise injury reduction. The Runna evidence is also unverified.
