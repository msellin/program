## What it means for Terav
- Threat: HYROX demand is large and growing, and sub-€10/mo HYROX-specific apps already exist at Terav's planned price (Victus, Crush). Runna has HYROX-partner status.
- Threat: the "race-specific plan" pitch is saturated. Terav would be the 10th-plus entrant, not the first.
- Gap (unverified): none of the retrieved listings clearly offer "fix one weak station, log-adaptive, alongside your gym week". Victus and Runna adapt to schedule, not to a station-level log. Treat as a hypothesis.
- Lesson: a HYROX station module would map onto existing Terav tracks (engine = SkiErg/row/run, strength = sled/carry/lunges, mobility) without a new product category.
- Lesson: do not copy race-plan framing. Terav's confirm-first, cited-study differentiator is the only visible wedge in the retrieved set.
