# Trust angle: AI plans and "evidence-based" claims (accessed 2026-10-27)

## Facts
- ABC Fitness Summer 2025 Wellness Watch (vendor survey): full trust in AI fitness apps is 43% Millennials, 33% Gen Z, 17% Boomers; nearly half report inaccurate AI data; 55% cite privacy. Sample size not reported. https://athletechnews.com/gen-zs-getting-gains-with-ai-but-boomers-just-arent-buying-it/ (its press release gives different Boomer figures, 12% and 2%: https://abcfitness.com/press-release/summer_wellness_watch_report/)
- Wachholz et al., BMC/PMC pilot (n=119 survey, 16 AI-plan users, 6 coach interviews, ChatGPT-3.5 half-marathon plan): AI-plan users had higher trust (p=0.030). Trust rating AI plan 3.73 vs human 3.36 (p=0.002). 4 of 6 coaches identified the AI plan. Self-report, tiny AI-user group, 3 institutions. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11908068/
- Juggernaut AI: marketed as an AI powerlifting app built on a named coach's method; not described as "science-based" in sources found. $34.99/mo per App Store tracker (July 2026). https://apppricinglab.com/iap/apple/1515756471 ; https://www.sensai.fit/blog/juggernaut-ai-vs-rp-hypertrophy-vs-alpha-progression-2026 (competitor blog, treat as self-interested)
- RP Hypertrophy: $299.99/yr per SensAI blog (Oct 2026); iOS launched US 2025-12-20. Adjusts sets from pump, soreness, workload feedback. https://www.sensai.fit/blog/juggernaut-ai-vs-rp-hypertrophy-vs-alpha-progression-2026 ; https://help.rpstrength.com/hc/en-us/articles/34725726510999-RP-Hypertrophy-App-What-s-new . No source found on its "evidence-based" framing.
- MacroFactor: App Store US listing $11.99/mo, $47.99/6mo, $71.99/yr; its own "proven nutrition and behavioral science" copy appears in a Saudi listing, i.e. developer marketing, not validation. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1553503471
- Stronger By Science: research site with paid coaching and 200k+ email list (https://strongerbyscience.com/about/). App pricing and trial not found.
- "You approve each change" as marketed by any named app: not found. Closest is a TrainerRoad forum user missing an accept/discard feature (anecdote only): https://www.trainerroad.com/forum/t/the-problem-with-new-ai-version-for-me/111157?page=2

## What it means for Terav
- Threat: AI-plan trust is split by age, and Boomers (a likely rehab/strength audience) mostly do not trust AI apps. Paid-AI competitors price at $300/yr or more, so a €89/yr product sits below the price anchor.
- Gap: no evidence found that "cites a study" moves purchase. "Science-based" is used by MacroFactor and others as generic copy, so it is noise unless the citation is specific and checkable.
- Gap: approve-each-change control is unmarketed in what was found, so it is an open differentiator, not a proven one.
- Lesson: lead with the control ("nothing changes without your Accept") and a visible reason per proposal, not with the word "evidence".
- Lesson: AI-plan trust rises with exposure (p=0.030 among users). The trial is the trust mechanism, so the 14-day trial matters more than copy.

Verdict signal: suggests-add:a visible per-proposal "why" with a log-signal and study link, plus Accept/Ignore control as the lead message.

Confidence: low. Only one peer-reviewed pilot (tiny, self-report) and one vendor survey (no sample size, conflicting figures). Prices come from aggregators and competitor blogs. No direct trust survey for science-cited training apps was found.
