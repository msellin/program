# H14 Meta ad policy check (access 2026-10-27)
Caveat: Meta Transparency Center pages are JS-rendered; fetch returned titles only. Rules below come from a secondary summary (https://www.accelerateddigitalmedia.com/insights/guide-to-social-media-health-ad-restrictions-2025/, accessed 2026-10-27) and vendor blogs. Re-check live pages before scaling.
Meta URLs confirmed to exist: https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness (accessed 2026-10-27). The personal-health-and-appearance URL returned 404 on the same date, so it appears retired or merged.
- [BLOCKS-ADS-TODAY] Pixel/CAPI health data: no event names or params that reveal pain, injury, rehab or programme stage (e.g. "pain_logged"). Health restrictions can attach at account/domain level and cap lower-funnel optimisation (Purchase, Lead). Source: secondary as above; Meta Business Tools Terms not fetched.
- [BLOCKS-ADS-TODAY] Ad-linked landing pages: no "pain", "injury", "rehab" or "your condition" copy. Ads must not assert or imply knowledge of a user's health (Personal Attributes; secondary as above).
- [BLOCKS-ADS-TODAY] Age: set targeting to 18+. Weight-loss and cosmetic rules require 18+ (secondary). Fitness services are listed as exempt general wellbeing (secondary). Whether a fitness app is classed as health and wellness is NOT confirmed by any fetched source; assume it may be.
- [BLOCKS-ADS-TODAY] No before/after or transformation imagery (secondary: permitted only for cosmetic products, 18+).
- [BEFORE-SCALE] Negative body image: no "statements of inferiority about physical appearance" (secondary). Applies to the headlines below.
- [BEFORE-SCALE] Customer and custom audience names and conversion event names must not imply health status (Business Tools Terms per vendor sources; not fetched).
Headline verdicts:
- "Your first strict pull-up in 8 weeks": HIGH risk. Second-person plus timeframe result claim. [BLOCKS-ADS-TODAY] Reword: "Build toward your first strict pull-up".
- "Fix your weakest lift": LOW-MED. Performance framing, but "your weakest" implies a personal deficit. [LATER] Test "Sharpen one lift at a time".
- "Stuck on muscle-ups?": MEDIUM. Question implies the user is failing. [BEFORE-SCALE] Reword: "Working on muscle-ups?"
- "Build your engine alongside CrossFit": LOW. No health claim. CrossFit trademark is outside Meta policy and not checked. [LATER]
- "Training plans backed by research": LOW-MED. Efficacy-type claim; keep only if each cited study supports it. [BEFORE-SCALE]
EU/global: GDPR Art. 9 treatment of pain check-in data is outside Meta policy and not checked here.
Not checked: Meta Business Tools Terms; Advertising Standards parent and Personal Attributes pages; Unacceptable Claims page; EU DSA ad rules; Events Manager data-source settings; the July 2026 reorganisation reported by a third-party tracker (unconfirmed); CrossFit trademark; GDPR.
