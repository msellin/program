## 3. What the evidence supports
- Paying users exist: H&F trial-to-paid ~35-40%, annual plans ~60% of subs (market-size, pricing-trials, retention-churn).
- No retrieved competitor shows a per-change cited reason with user Accept. Fitbod is criticised for prescribing without explaining (fitbod, future-and-ai-coaches, whoop, evidence-trust-angle). This is an absence claim from thin searches.
- Single-goal framing is unclaimed by the big full-plan products (crossfit-programming, ladder, fitbod, hybrid-hyrox); Runna proves single-goal plans sell (runna).
- Price is not a barrier: Terav is below most adaptive coaches (runna, fitbod, ladder, pliability).
- Competitor complaints match Terav's design: rigid plans (ladder, gmb), unexplained plans (fitbod), billing surprise (fitbod, pliability) (user-pain-points is thin).
- Joint work is viable only as a training constraint, not treatment (joint-prehab-apps).

## 5. Threats
- Free or near-free: Hevy Trainer ~$3, Splash/ErgData/Pete plan for erg, Calisteniapp 500+ routines, one-off courses $4-299. Terav must win on adaptation, not content. BEFORE-SCALE.
- Whoop and Fitbod already sell "adapts to your log/recovery". Focus, not adaptation, is the differentiator. BLOCKS-ADS (ad copy).
- Direct skill rivals: Thenx, Movement Athlete (assess then place, 100+ skills), Madbarz. Muscle-up/handstand ads meet them. BEFORE-SCALE.
- Goal-reached churn: H&F first-renewal 30%; Terav's design ends in a goal. BEFORE-SCALE.
- Ad-policy: joint/rehab wording, health claims. Primary Meta/Google text not read. BLOCKS-ADS (read it, keep copy to "train around it").

## 6. Not established
- No demand test. No CAC or cost per trial for fitness in EUR. No EU data. Reddit/pain-point text unverified. Search volume for most goals missing (goal-demand). Retention for single-goal apps missing. Whether "cites a study" moves anyone: no evidence. Web-checkout vs store conversion: not found. Most price rows are snippets.
- Break-even (my arithmetic, assumptions): EU VAT ~21-24% comes off 9.99, leaving ~8.1-8.3; web fee 5-10% leaves ~7.3-7.8/mo (ignoring VAT: 9.0-9.5). Apple 30% case in acquisition-costs was 6.99. Allow 3 paid months: ~€22 net (€27 if VAT ignored). Annual €89: ~€67 net after VAT and fee.
  Max cost per trial = CAC x trial-to-paid: ~€8.4 at 38% (up to ~€10 ignoring VAT). If renewal is only 30%, expected months fall to ~2 and the ceiling to ~€6. So the web fee moves break-even only ~€1-2 versus Apple; trial-to-paid and renewal matter far more.
- €150 test: at an assumed €5-8 per trial (unsourced), 19-30 trials; at ~€4 CPI and 9.5% install-to-trial it could be as few as 4-10. Expect 7-12 paid at best, and the paid count lags the 14-day trial. Below Meta's ~50 events/week learning level.
  It cannot tell 25% from 40% conversion (needs ~150 trials per arm). It CAN show: cost per trial start against a ~€6-8 ceiling; whether a cold click will start a trial at all; and a near-zero outcome (0 paid of ~20 trials has p<0.001 at 38%).
- Only the smoke test answers: will cold traffic start a trial for one named goal; which goal copy (pull-up vs muscle-up vs handstand vs rowing) pulls; trial-to-paid; which message (explanation/Accept vs outcome) lands.
