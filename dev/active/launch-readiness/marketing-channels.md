# Marketing channels beyond the €150 ad test (owner idea list, 2026-10-30)

Source: the owner's channel list for another product (a betting-tips service), mapped onto Terav. Roadmap item
**M-08** in `ACTION-POINTS.md`. None of this starts before the ad test (M-05) is read (M-06): that read says what a
sign-up and an activation are worth, which decides how much any paid channel below may cost.

Rules that carry over from the rest of the plan: no health claims, no medical framing, never the owner's clinical
data or the hip track in public material, figures and screens come from the persona harness, never a real account.

6/8 (daily figure posts) → 2 (directories, once) → 1/3 (owner outreach) → 4/15 (paid, sized by M-06).
