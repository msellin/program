# "Every track is backed by research": can we sell it, and when? (2026-10-27)

**Owner idea:** a strong selling point is that every track is proven by the whitepapers and university research papers.
**Verdict:** **yes, it's sellable as the trust layer under the outcome, not as the headline, and not as "proven".**
It gets louder in stages, and each stage is gated on the evidence actually holding up.

## What is true today
- **Whitepapers:** 8 internal ones (`dev/whitepapers/00-07`), around 25+ primary sources each. They are Terav's own
  synthesis, not university publications.
- **Citations:** `citations.json` holds **125** entries, drawn from published, mostly university literature.
- **Problems with them:**
  - About **25 are rated overstated** and **13 can't be found** (traffic-plan hold, still open, `roadmap-reconciliation.md`).
  - Withdrawn ones still ship (Sadowski, kilding_2012).
  - **0 of 9 programs are VERIFIED** (all are "CITED").
- **Research supports components, not whole programs.** Studies back the methods inside a program (zone-2 work,
  practice spacing, progression). No study shows that *Terav's* pull-up program produces a pull-up, so "proven"
  would be false.
- **Market signal** (`market/evidence-trust-angle.md`): "science-based" is generic competitor copy (MacroFactor,
  RP, Juggernaut). **Nobody markets "every change shows its reason and you approve it".** That mechanism is the
  part no one else has. Citations are the proof behind it.
- **Ad policy:** Google treats "research-backed" as a claim that needs support (Unreliable claims /
  Misrepresentation). Meta bans implied guaranteed results.
- **Lesson from Plain Words:** correct is not enough. Evidence is a reason to trust, not a reason to click. Lead
  with the outcome; the evidence closes the sale.

## Stages
| Stage | When | What to say | Gate before moving on |
|-------|------|-------------|------------------------|
| **1. Quiet proof** | Hackathon / first ads | Under the outcome: "Built on published sport science. Every change shows its reason and its source." Program pages show the sources behind *that* program. No counts, no "proven", no "university" | Overclaims stripped (B4); withdrawn citations out (B12) |
| **2. Verified badge** | Before scale (~1–2 weeks) | Per program: "Verified: every source checked against what it claims." Make the whitepapers readable public pages (also SEO content). The evidence page shows real counts | Traffic hold closed; `evidence-verifier` run on all 125; a program earns VERIFIED only when every source holds |
| **3. Expert review** | After the first paying users | "Reviewed by [named coach / sport scientist / physio, with credential]." A real person with a real credential is the strongest trust signal, and ad policies accept it | A paid, named reviewer signs off on the advertised programs |
| **4. Own outcomes** | ~3–6 months of data | "Beta athletes added a median of N strict pull-ups in 8 weeks" | Enough consenting users, an honest method, and ad-policy wording review. This beats any citation |

## Action points
- **HACKATHON:** use the Stage-1 line (B4/B5); per-program "Sources" disclosure on the program pages; never "proven",
  "clinically", "university" or "125 studies".
- **BEFORE-SCALE:** close the traffic hold → `evidence-verifier` on all citations → VERIFIED badges → publish the
  whitepapers as pages.
- **LATER:** recruit a named expert reviewer (budget, owner); an outcome-data pipeline (consented, aggregated).
