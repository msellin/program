# Terav — how to use it

One page. Hand this to someone with the app open.

Written 2026-09-13 from what the app actually does, not from the landing copy.
Where the two disagree, this is the one that was checked.

---

## What it is

You pick **one** focus — an engine, a skill, a lift, a stubborn joint — and
Terav sharpens that arc against what you log. It runs **alongside** your
existing week.

It is not a full training plan. If you want something that tells you what to do
on all seven days, this is the wrong app.

---

## The first five minutes

1. **Sign up.** You will be asked to accept the terms and to consent to storing
   symptom data. The second one is not optional — the app cannot work without
   it, because symptoms are what the engine reads.
2. **Pick a programme** from the catalog. Sort by shortest or easiest if you are
   not sure.
3. **Answer the intake.** Some questions are safety gates — answer them
   honestly, because a wrong answer there is the one that matters. Physical
   tests are optional; skip them if you have no bar to hand.
4. **Do the morning check.** One minute of tapping. Until you do it, the engine
   has nothing to work with.

---

## Every day

**Morning check first.** Four or five taps: how each area feels, how life is
going. It takes under a minute and it is the single input the whole engine runs
on.

The check produces one of three states:

| | what it means | what the app does |
|---|---|---|
| **Green** | nothing above 3/10 | run the plan as written |
| **Amber** | something is stiff or sore, or life is heavy | may propose taking load off |
| **Red** | pain above 5/10, night pain, gait change | back off; the session is not the priority today |

**Then open the session.** Log sets as you do them. Tap a set to change the
weight or reps if what you did was not what it said.

**If something hurts mid-session**, use `⋯ → Something hurt`. It records the
area on today's log and shows you the rule you agreed to at intake. It does not
end your session for you — that stays your call.

---

## When the engine proposes something

You will see an amber card on Day. It might say your training max should go up,
or that today should be lighter, or that you have earned the next tier.

**Nothing happens until you tap Accept.** That is the whole mechanic. The engine
proposes, you decide.

Every proposal tells you *why* — a study it is citing, or the signal in your own
log it is reading. Tap the card to see it. If it is wrong, tap Ignore and it
goes away.

Accepted something by mistake? **Undo** appears for six seconds on the card.

---

## What to log, and what not to bother with

**Log:** working sets with weight and reps, sessions you did elsewhere
(`Off-plan → Lifts you did elsewhere`), runs and rows, and anything that hurt.

**Do not bother:** warm-ups, and precise rep counts on accessory work. The
engine reads top sets, symptoms and your notes. Notes are free text — "felt
strong", "shoulder grumbly on the last set" — and it does read them.

**The one thing worth being strict about:** if you train outside the app, log
it. The engine cannot see your CrossFit class, and it will happily propose a
heavier top set the day after a session it knows nothing about.

---

## What it will not do

- It will not give you medical advice. If something is genuinely wrong, see a
  physio. The app says so and means it.
- It will not plan your whole week.
- It will not change your training maxes on its own. Ever.

---

## When something is broken or confusing

**Profile → Send feedback.** It goes to a person, not a queue, and replies come
back to the address you signed up with.

That includes "this proposal made no sense" — that is the most useful kind.
