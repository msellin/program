# Mobile UX — Plan tab
Score now: 4/10 (Plan, opened-days state). What a 10 needs: one open day at a time, so the screen holds one day's detail. One compact primary action per open day with no wrapped label. Move/Skip out of the content column. Verbs that cannot apply are hidden. Track rows that are the log entry points.
Record Recent score: 6/10 (not examined in depth).
D1 APPROVE — phone-3 shows one open past day costing ~390px (about 46% of an 852px viewport) with 2 tracks. Several open days (phone-1..6) means 6 or more screens of scroll. Accordion keeps the cost to one day. Scroll the opened row into view under the sticky area after it opens, or the row jumps off-screen when the previous one collapses.
D2 APPROVE — "Log session →" wraps to 3 lines at 393pt in grid-cols-3 (page.tsx:897, phone-3). A single `w-full min-h-12` button fixes it. Keep the label short ("Log", "Preview", "Open day"). Do not put the bronze fill on past or future days.
D3 AMEND — choose (a), a "⋯" on the open day's header row, but make it a 44x44 target (`size-11`) at the right edge, beside the chevron. It opens a bottom sheet (ConfirmSheet already exists, :946), so Move/Skip land in the thumb zone. (c) acts on a "selected" day far from the finger, in the ouch zone. (b) leaves the verbs in the content column. The "⋯" sits at the row header, which is mid-screen, not in the top third. Move/Skip are rare actions, so they should cost one extra tap.
D4 APPROVE — Move/Skip on a logged day (Mon in phone-3) is dead weight and risks a mis-tap. Hiding them removes 1 of 3 buttons. Keep Move on a missed day.
D5 AMEND — go with tappable track rows. Each row is `min-h-12`, shows "Anterior Hip Rebuild · Not logged ›", and has a full-row hit area. The sheet alternative adds a tap and a layer for 2 targets. Rows replace both the "Log overhead mobility →" full-width button (:873-887) and "Log session". Keep at least 8px between rows. A single-track day keeps one row, so it is not a regression.
D6 APPROVE — "planned" beside a "Missed" badge (phone-3, Tue) contradicts itself. Use "Not logged" in muted amber and a check when done.
D7 AMEND — option (b) is the better choice, but verify Recent still has content on Monday. If it would be empty, fall back to (a). Recent is not a Plan duplicate, since it spans weeks. Dropping the current week stops the same day showing on both tabs.
D8 APPROVE (keep Sentence case) — the 4-item nav has ample width. Uppercase at ~12px is harder to read for no gain. Active state is already color plus a bronze top bar, which works (phone-3).
D9 (own) — the sticky bottom nav covers the last ~64px of content (phone-3: the Wed row is clipped). Check that the Plan scroll container has `pb-[calc(env(safe-area-inset-bottom)+5rem)]`, and that the open-day scroll-into-view from D1 clears the nav.
Top 3 findings:
1. page.tsx:897 — grid-cols-3 wraps "Log session →" into 3 lines (phone-3). Move/Skip cost equal visual weight to the primary action, and 3 buttons plus a 4th full-width button stack to ~170px per day.
2. page.tsx:67-75 — multi-open `Set` with nothing open by default. Accordion plus Today open on load removes the main scroll cost.
