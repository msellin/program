# 13 Trainer review: handstand family (hand-balancing coach, wall-work to walking)
Evidence: angles.md rows (trunk only is measured for most hold figures, so arm/shoulder/hip lines are judged from sheets 05-07). Norms used: wall HS chest-in hands 15-25 cm off the wall (~5-8 deg line); freestanding line within +-3 deg, shoulders open ~180; tuck HS hips stay over hands, hip included ~50-80, knee ~30-40; HS walk step ~20-30 cm, ~1 s/step; kick-up lunge start, front knee ~120-140, swing leg to vertical in ~0.4-0.6 s.

### wristprep — 7/10
- [M] Same figure serves hs_wrist_pushups_floor but it only rocks; a push-up needs elbow 180 -> ~90 and back, trunk held 85-90 → add a variant or stop mapping it.
- [M] Rock reads fine (trunk 89-90, hip/knee 88 -> 113, shin vertical) but shoulder travel past the wrists is unmeasured → shoulders 5-10 cm (~10-15 deg of arm from vertical) past the wrists, wrist extension ~70-80 deg, hips following, not folding.
- [L] 6 s per rock is a good slow tempo; add a short pause at the front end (~0.5 s).

### fingerraise — 7/10
- [L] Everything is static except the fingers (hip 96, trunk 90) which is right; make sure the finger lift (~30-45 deg off the floor, palm stays down) is readable at 144 px.
- [L] 4 s cycle: lift ~1 s, hold ~1 s, lower ~1.5 s feels right.

### downdog — 8/10
- [L] Trunk 36 deg from vertical (torso ~54 deg above floor) is a touch upright for a wrist-relief dog → hips high, shoulder-hip line ~40-45 deg from vertical; arms and torso one straight line (shoulder ~170-180), heels reaching down, head between the arms.
- [L] Add a subtle breathing sway; hold has no life.

### child — 8/10
- [L] Hip 18, knee 27, trunk 84 is correct (hips to heels, chest low); arms forward, shoulder flexion ~150-170, head resting not lifted → make sure the head sits down between/ahead of the arms.

### wallhs — 8/10
- [L] Trunk 6 deg is right for chest-in (hands 15-25 cm off the wall); keep it, a hair of hollow (ribs in, ~0-5 deg) is the coaching point. Add a slow +-1 deg toe-press sway so it reads as an active hold.

### wallhs_90 — 6/10
- [M] Name says "90" (a vertical, 90 deg to the floor) but trunk is 15 deg; tile shows hands a step off the wall with the body leaning → decide: if it is the vertical hold, trunk 0-5 deg with feet resting on the wall; if it is the "step away" hold keep 15 but rename. Today it duplicates wallhs visually.
- [L] Arms must read straight with open shoulders (~180); not measured.

### wallhs_lean — 7/10
- [M] Trunk 38 deg; if "135" means 135 deg included with the floor, target ~45 deg from vertical (hands ~1.2 m from the wall, much further than the tile shows). Progression 6 -> 15 -> 38 is monotonic, which is right; check the wall distance matches the lean.
- [L] Shoulders should stay open (no sag at the shoulder line).

### wallhs_shrug — 6/10
- [H] Trunk is constant 6 and no shoulder or scapular channel is measured; on the sheets the shrug (shoulders rising to the ears and a small sink) is not visible → scapular elevation ~10-15% of the arm length, arms locked elbow 180 throughout, ~1 s up, hold 0.5 s, ~1.5 s down, body line unchanged.

### wallhs_taps — 6/10
- [H] Work arm starts at elbow 147 and shoulder 163; the start must be arms locked → elbow 175-180, shoulder ~170-180, the support arm locked throughout (the sheet shows the support arm also folding).
- [M] Tap reaches elbow 24 (hand on the opposite shoulder); fine, but the weight shift onto the support hand (hips/shoulders over it, ~2-3 cm lateral, not visible in a side view) is the whole point → show the body line shifting ~3-5 deg toward the support hand before the hand leaves the floor.
- [L] Tap and return should be a controlled ~1 s each, not a flick.

### hsfree — 8/10
- [L] Trunk 0-3 deg with sway is realistic balance correction; shoulders open (~180) and fingers loaded not measured. Add a ~0.5 cm finger-pad press at the peaks of the sway, the visible "corrections through the fingers".

### hstuck — 6/10
- [H] Tuck is too shallow: hip 180 -> 105, knee 180 -> 30 → hip included ~55-75 (knees to chest, thighs ~100-120 deg of flexion), knee ~30-40; knees close before hips opens is correct, keep.
- [M] Tile shows the legs poking out horizontally above the trunk like a pike; shin 72 deg from vertical is too flat → shins ~30-50 deg from vertical with knees stacked over/behind the hips, and the shoulders push open as the knees come in.
- [L] 6 s cycle is OK; hold the tuck ~1 s.

### hswalk — 5/10
- [H] Only the trunk is measured and it is constant 2 deg; a walking step needs the body line to shift toward the support hand (trunk lean ~3-6 deg in the walking direction at the step, back to ~0-2 after) and the hands travel ~20-30 cm per step → add lean at weight shift; the sheet frames are almost identical.
- [M] Step tempo ~1 s per step (3.6 s cycle is two steps, good); legs counterbalance with a small split (~10-20 deg), shoulders open throughout.
- [M] Hand placement: leading hand reaches before the weight shifts, trailing hand then catches up (not simultaneous).

### kickup — 6/10
- [H] Start is not a kick-up setup: planted-leg hip included 24 (chest on thigh) and swing leg already horizontal at t=0 → lunge start, front knee ~120-140, hip ~60-80, trunk 45-60, swing leg low behind (~20-30 deg under horizontal) then drives.
- [M] Swing leg should accelerate (~0.4-0.6 s) to vertical, plant leg pushes off and joins, hips stack over the hands; 4.5 s cycle is fine but the up phase should be quick, the stack held ~1 s, then the controlled return.
- [M] Return at 75% is trunk 45 with legs coming down the same path; a coach shows a pike-down or a step-down with the legs together, not a mirrored swing.

### wallwalk — 6/10
- [M] Start trunk 71 deg from vertical; a plank with feet at the wall base should be 80-88 deg → hands further out, body straight (hip 175-180).
- [M] Motion is a smooth slide; the real movement is steps (feet up ~15-20 cm and hands in ~15-20 cm alternately, ~6-8 steps) → stepped easing with pauses.
- [L] End trunk 20 deg is good (hands ~25-30 cm off the wall, chest to the wall); hold ~1-2 s then walk down.

## Lowest scoring
hswalk 5; wallhs_shrug, wallhs_taps, hstuck, kickup, wallwalk, wallhs_90 at 6.
