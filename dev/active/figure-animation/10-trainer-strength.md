Gait figures (sled, suitcase, farmer) only report trunk in angles.md; leg/arm numbers for carries are read off the sheet.

### frontsquat — 8/10

- [L] Rack: upper arm 102-115 deg from trunk is about horizontal (elbows high), good. Elbow 33 fixed, fine.

### airsquat — 8/10

### goblet — 7/10
- [M] Bottom hip 69 / knee 66 is only parallel; a goblet squat is taught as the deep pattern (elbows inside knees) → hip 55-62, knee 55-62, trunk 20-30, shin 28-35.
- [L] Trunk 22 and elbow 65 (elbows down) good; add a slight hold at bottom (pry) since 'knees out' is in the label.

### deadlift — 4/10
- [H] Start hip 35 / knee 82 is impossible for a mid-shin pull (thigh crushed onto torso, deeper than a full squat) → hip 70-90, knee 110-130, trunk 40-50 from vertical, shin 15-20 (knees forward to meet the bar).
- [H] Never reaches lockout: max hip 168, knee 155 and 50% pose hip 155 / knee 151 while the label says "stand tall" → hip and knee 175-180, trunk 0-3 at the top, glutes squeezed, no lean back.
- [H] Lowering at 75% (trunk 53, hip 47, knee 90) is a squat down, knees bending first → lower is a hinge: hips travel back with knees ~160 until the bar passes the knees, then knees bend, trunk reaching 45-50 at the plates.
- [M] Shin 8-13 throughout; for the bar to start at mid-shin the shin must start ~15-20 and move back toward ~5-8 as the knees extend (knees clear the bar path).
- [M] Trunk 55 → 16 by 25%: the back angle opens too early. A good pull keeps trunk angle nearly constant until the bar passes the knees (hips and shoulders rise together), then the hips drive through.

### bridge2 — 6/10

- [M] Start hip 123-127 with trunk 90: hips are not fully down on the floor/pelvis rests with legs too flexed; at start use hip ~100-110 with the knee 100-115.
- [M] Top hip 178-180 (full) is correct, but cue "no hyperextension" and ribs down; keep max 175-180, trunk line ~15-25 deg above the floor (currently 23, good).

### hipthrust — 8/10

### splitsquat — 6/10

- [M] Front leg at start: hip 143 / knee 167 / shin 14 is inconsistent with a standing split stance → hip 165-175, knee 170-178 (just short of lock), front shin ~5-10; bottom hip 80 / knee 87 / shin ~0-10 is right.

### slrdl — 5/10
- [H] Stance knee flexes 155 → 130 (and the stance shin moves out to 11-14 deg) → an RDL is a hinge: stance knee ~160-170 throughout (soft, ~10-20 flexion), shin stays ~0-5, hip travels back not down.
- [H] Stance hip reaches 73 included at trunk 70 → the stance thigh is pitching forward; target stance thigh near vertical, hip included ~110-120 at the bottom.
- [M] Top is not locked: hip 167 / knee 155 → 175-180, then glute squeeze; trunk 0.
- [M] Trunk 70 is acceptable (back-leg-and-torso line ~15-20 deg above horizontal); allow up to 80 if hamstrings are fine, but hips stay square (no opening).
- [L] Free leg in line with torso (shin = trunk = 70): correct, the figure shows it.

### nordic — 7/10

- [M] Lowering speed profile is good (2, 18, 49, 68: starts slow, then accelerates), but at 8 s the eccentric is too long for a model rep; real rep: ~3-5 s down.

### sled — 5/10
- [H] Arms: elbow 105-114 included with shoulder ~80 (bent) while the label says "arms long" → elbow 165-175, shoulder 110-130 (hands on high posts, arms in line with the trunk).
- [H] Drive leg never extends: max hip 144, knee 133 → at push-off hip 165-175, knee 165-175, with ankle-knee-hip-shoulder in one line (lean 45).
- [M] Trailing shin reaches 85 deg from vertical (foot kicked up behind like a sprint) → heavy sled steps are short and low; swing foot clears ~10-20 cm only, shin ~50-60 max.

### suitcase — 7/10
- [M] Only trunk is measured (0, fixed); coaches want a visible "resist lean": shoulders level, load-side shoulder not dropped, free arm calm (no swing). Verify drawing: free arm should hold still.
- [M] Cycle 2.4 s = 1.2 s per step is slow for a walk; carry cadence ~0.5-0.6 s per step → cycle ~1.0-1.2 s, steps short and at hip-width.
- [L] Weight stays at the thigh without swinging, hip-width stance: keep.

### farmer — 7/10

- [L] Arms hang long at the sides with the bells at mid-thigh, shoulders packed and level: right. Add a subtle breathing/ribs-down motion if frames allow.
