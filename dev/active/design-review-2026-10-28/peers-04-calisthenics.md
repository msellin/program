## Peer evidence by screen type

**Onboarding / assessment**
- Freeletics: long quiz (~25 steps) with a fitness-level slider, a "Building your plan" animation, then Training Journey
  choice. Paywall follows sign-up. High density, interactive. https://screensdesign.com/showcase/freeletics-workouts-fitness
  Coach built from goal/background answers: https://www.freeletics.com/en/blog/posts/getting-started-with-freeletics/
- Calistack: two-question assessment places you at a start difficulty. https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760246808
- Thenx: skill-level selection, then workout of the day (from a review). https://apps.apple.com/app/thenx/id1192413645
- Terav's pull-up intake (16 steps, tier labels) is the same idea but text-only; peers add motion/slider and a payoff screen.

**Home / today**
- Freeletics Coach tab: scheduled session card with duration, equipment, focus, target muscles; Start / "Adapt session"
  buttons; a calendar strip of solid circles for training days; streak counter and "perfect week" countdown top-left.
- Caliverse: "your session, planned for the day" view, calendar, streak. https://www.caliverse.app/
- Calisteniapp: calendar/agenda showing current phase and session, iOS widgets (today/week/month), Apple Watch.
  https://apps.apple.com/app/calisteniapp/id1265489771
- Thenx: daily recommended workout, "Workout of the day". Density (unverified): low, card-led.

**Workout / session player**
- Thenx and Caliverse: exercise video demo before/while starting (HD technique video + form cues, 600+ exercises).
  https://www.caliverse.app/ , https://apps.apple.com/app/thenx/id1192413645
- Freeletics: video per exercise, always warm-up then cool-down. Calisteniapp: customizable warm-up/cool-down, Watch control.
- Complaints show what to avoid: no in-workout timer, workout closing on app switch, tap "Next" after every exercise,
  can't see last time's numbers (Thenx, Calisteniapp, Caliverse reviews, same URLs).
- Caliverse Smart Coach shows PBs and "how you trained last time" per exercise, and "level up to a harder variation".
  https://apps.apple.com/app/id1472004214

**Exercise detail**
- Thenx: exercise and technique previews inside Personal Goals. Calisteniapp: related-content discovery from exercise
  screens. Caliverse: HD video + form cues. Calistack: video on every movement. Skill-Map: description, prerequisites, "leads to".
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/skill-map/id6761576511

**Progress and graphs**
- Calisteniapp: weekly/monthly stats, training-load index, PR screens (incl. 1-5RM estimate, tonnage), muscle map of most-trained
  groups, workout photos in records. Charts' type unverified.
- Thenx: muscle analytics / activation view, rep/set/PB logging.
- Freeletics: Daily Athlete Score on Profile (performance, consistency, skill level; best of last 90 days; compared to peers).
- Skill-Map/Calistack: visual progress on the tree; "share your progression as an image" (Calistack).
- Terav has one heatmap; no per-skill chart, PB line, or share image.

**Program catalog / program page**
- Calisteniapp: 40+ programs, phase previews, program pause. Freeletics: "Training Journey" compare-and-pick, 6-12 weeks.
- Caliverse: structured multi-week plans by level, from first pull-up to planche. Thenx: beginner-to-advanced programs.
- Imagery/thumbnail style unverified; assume photo/video thumbnails (not evidenced).

**Completion / reward**
- Calisteniapp: achievements screen with 3D-viewable badges, unlocked-exercises section, shareable routine images.
- Freeletics: badges, streaks, skill progressions in listing: https://apps.apple.com/app/654810212 (via search result).
- Caliverse: streak, monthly challenges with prizes. Terav: no completion screen was seen in the viewed set.

**Body maps / scores / levels / skill trees**
- Skill-Map: pinch-and-pan interactive graph of 50+ skills across 6 categories; tap for prerequisites; mark achieved,
  set target, log PBs (reps or hold time). Reviewer says stages not clear enough.
- Calistack: tree in Push/Pull/Core/Legs, auto-unlock when requirements are met, pulsing animation toward the next skill.
- CaliFlow: built around skill tree and filming your journey (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.skilltree.app).
  Lever: 80+ skills, 5 categories (https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760668679, listing only).
- Calisteniapp / Thenx: muscle map. Freeletics: Athlete Score. Calisteniapp's skill-tree claim is from an unreliable page; not counted.

## The 10 elements Terav should adopt first (ranked)
1. Exercise demo loops (short silent video or illustrated loop) on Session player and exercise detail. Peers all lead with it.
2. Visual level ladder replacing "Hang -> Assisted -> First Rep -> Volume" text on Program detail: stepped nodes, current
   one highlighted, locked/next states, auto-unlock on criteria (Calistack pattern).
3. "You are here" placement result at end of Intake: show the ladder with the user's node lit plus one-line why.
4. Session preview card on Today: duration, focus, 3 exercise thumbnails, one big Start; Terav rest state keeps one line, not a paragraph.
5. Per-skill progress chart on Record (dead-hang seconds / best reps over weeks) with PB marker, above the heatmap.
6. Week-circle strip + streak counter on Today (Freeletics/Caliverse model), filled by Sessions done.
7. Hero imagery or illustration on program cards and detail (Programs carousel, Program detail), replacing text-only cards.
8. Post-session completion screen: PB callout, ladder progress tick, next-session preview, share-as-image.
9. Intake payoff: short "Building your plan" interstitial plus a progress bar with section names, shorter question wording (16 steps is above peer norms except Freeletics).
10. Badges for ladder milestones (first dead hang 30s, first rep) on Profile; 3D optional, flat bronze fine.

Caveat: items 1-3 are what the pull-up/handstand ad stranger will compare first. Verify visuals against real screenshots before building.
