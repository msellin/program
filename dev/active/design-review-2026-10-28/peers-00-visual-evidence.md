## What every peer has that Terav has none of
1. **Movement imagery on every exercise**, as a photo, video loop or illustrated figure. Hevy uses small figures, Calis cut-out photos, GOWOD and Thenx video.
2. **One hero visual per screen**: a score ring (GOWOD), a photo card (pliability), a plan progress card (Runna) or a chart (Hevy).
3. **Charts with selectable metrics** (Hevy line chart plus metric chips; GOWOD bars and sparklines).
4. **Icons on choices and shortcuts** (Runna goal emoji, pliability tiles, GOWOD body icons).
5. **Very low words per element**: a title plus at most one helper line, never paragraphs.
6. **Week and progress indicators as graphics** (pliability dots, Runna segmented bar, Runna calendar colour bars).
