## What improved
- Program page (05-program-detail) now opens on a figure hero, a title, one pitch line and three big stat tiles, with a sticky bronze Start free. That is Pliability's shape (hero, title, CTA) with far fewer words than the old section cards.
- Quick start (06-intake-01): 3 steps with a "STEP 1 OF 3" label and one question per screen. It is shorter than the 16 steps and reads like Runna's goal question. The long path is demoted to "Detailed setup (about 5 min)".
- The Today day-1 tour card is gone. The first card is "Start your first session" with a bronze primary, which matches the peer pattern of one action on arrival.
- The catalog card (06-programs-top) has a figure thumbnail, so it begins to read as a shelf instead of a directory.
