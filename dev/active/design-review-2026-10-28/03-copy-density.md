# 03 Copy density and clarity (lens: text volume, jargon, one-thing-per-screen)
**Verdict:** Today, Record and the red rest card are lean and clear. The ad path leaks in two places: the program-detail page and the intake, where "Tier A/B" and "sub-capability" reach a stranger.
Scores (1-5, 5 = good): text density 3 | text size 4 | visual richness 3 | consistency 3
Paths: A = dev/active/launch-readiness/screens/gaps/A-first-run/, C = .../gaps/C-viewport-375/, P = next-app/tests/e2e/artifacts/personas/
## Findings
1. **ADS-BLOCKER: intake answers show "TIER A / A / B / B / C".** A-first-run/06-intake-01.png and 06-intake-03.png. The tier label sits under every answer and means nothing to a stranger. It also reveals the scoring and invites gaming the answer. Cut it from the UI. Rewrite the answers to plain speech: "Can't hang 15 s yet", "Hang 15 s+, no reps", "Band-assisted reps".
2. **ADS-BLOCKER: the 03-intake question is 41 words.** 06-intake-03.png. "How many strict feet-elevated ring rows can you do (feet on a box, body horizontal, pull chest to rings)?" plus a 30-word proxy explainer. Rewrite to "Ring rows, feet up: how many?", with a 12-word helper: "Body flat, chest to rings. A barbell row in a rack counts."
3. **FIX-THIS-WEEK: the program detail page is a wall of copy.** A-first-run/05-program-detail.png; P/persona-pullup/mobile/07-programs-active.png. It has 4 numbered sections of about 40 words each (about 250 words), a 3-line intro, and a "Levels:" chain that repeats the arc. "SECTION 1 OF 4" is an editorial label, not user value. Drop it. "Multi-tier skill program on the shared pull drill library. Grip → scap → negative → band…" is internal jargon. Keep one line: "From no hang to a rep you own." Collapse sections 3 and 4 behind "How it works". Lead with a visual: the 4 levels as a stepper, with the user's level highlighted. "Baseline setup — a few minutes on the wizard + a 3-day measurement window" reads as a heavy ask. Say "Set up: 16 questions, about 3 min".
4. **FIX-THIS-WEEK: the intake helper copy mixes English and jargon.** Examples are "Personalised… sub-capability", "used to shape band-tension progression" (06-intake-10.png) and "AND 'WHERE YOU ARE…'" in the header. The header at 375 wraps awkwardly: "WHERE YOU ARE… · STEP 3 OF 16" is truncated, and the step count shows twice (header and footer "3 / 16"). Keep one counter. Name the bodyweight question for the user's benefit: "Bodyweight (kg). Picks your band strength."
5. **FIX-THIS-WEEK: the first-run Today screen stacks four things.** A-first-run/07-today-day-1.png. It stacks the "Four tabs" explainer (4 lines, 35 words), a setup card, the session and "Log an extra session". The tab labels already say Today/Plan/Record/Profile. Delete the tab tour. The Got it button costs a tap for no gain.
6. **FIX-THIS-WEEK: the session brief stacks three "how to run this" cards before the Start button.** A-first-run/08-first-session.png. Each card is a bare sentence ("Feet-elevated ring row / inverted row.") with no label saying what it is. The footer "HANG, SCAP, ROW BASE · WEEK 1 OF 8 · ENDS 22 DEC" is monospace jargon (3 lines). Show one exercise list (name plus sets) and the single line "5 exercises, about 32 min". The "Follow along" blocks repeat "guided, one at a time, with a timer" three times (A-first-run/09-first-session-bottom.png). Say it once.
7. **FIX-THIS-WEEK: coach and tier terms leak onto Today and Plan.** P/persona-handstand/mobile/01-day.png; C/02-plan-top.png. "SUGGESTION: MOVE UP A TIER. Because: Your latest retest clears Tier B — Wall handstand established's threshold. Advancing swaps your weekly focus to the next tier's drills. Prep + recovery blocks stay." is 38 words, and "established's threshold" is broken grammar. Rewrite: "You held a wall handstand. Move up to Level 2?" with "Source: Rhea 2003" as a chip. The button label is 8 words; shorten it to "Move up". Plan: "Mixed practice · Post-acquisition: engine interleaves 2-3 drills per session across the weakest capabilities." is jargon. Use "Each session mixes 2-3 drills on your weakest areas." "tier", "arc", "engine" and "capability" appear in user copy. Pick "level" for tier and drop the others from the UI.
8. **LATER: citation clutter is mild.** A "CITED" pill sits on program cards and detail pages (A-first-run/04-programs.png, 05-program-detail.png). It is unexplained and could read as an academic gimmick. Show it only on proposals, where the source name does the job. Record's one-liner "Every change cites a study or a signal from your log" is good, but it sits on Record, not at the moment of proposal.
9. **LATER: tone is mostly right, with two leaks.** P/persona-strength-long/mobile/01-day.png: "57 weeks logged. Nice." and "Keep going 4 more weeks" are mild streak copy. The "How was this program?" box is unrelated to the ads path.
10. **LATER: Record is dense but scannable.** P/persona-pullup/mobile/05b-record.png. The heatmaps carry the data. The 30-row log below is fine because it is collapsed per day. "1 green · 1 amber · 1 red" wraps badly at 375 (C/04-record-top.png). Use colored dots only.
