# 02 Mobile UX (375/393): above-the-fold, scroll, tap targets, sticky bars, thumb reach

**Verdict:** Good enough for the ad test. The in-session flow is strong and thumb-friendly. The Today first-run stack and the session brief push the key action down or crowd it with a heavy sticky footer, and that is worth a quick fix.
Scores (1-5): text density 3, text size 4, visual richness 3, consistency 4.

## Findings
1. **FIX-THIS-WEEK: the Day-1 Today screen puts the first-run chrome above the session.** At 375 you see the title, a progress bar, a 5-line "Four tabs" explainer card, and only the top of "Week 1. Start your first session" before the nav cuts it off at y=614. The one action a stranger needs, starting the session, is below the fold, behind a dismissible tutorial. Fix: collapse "Four tabs" to a one-line chip, or show it after the first session. Evidence: `dev/active/launch-readiness/screens/gaps/A-first-run/07-today-day-1.png`.
2. **FIX-THIS-WEEK: the session brief has a sticky footer that eats about 28% of the viewport.** "Log an extra session", a mono caption (a 2-line phase line, "HANG, SCAP, ROW BASE · WEEK 1 OF 8 · ENDS 22 DEC"), the 48px Start button and the 53px nav stack up. At 375x667 that leaves about 390px of scrolling content, and it clips a card mid-line (the Follow-along card at y~450). The caption is non-actionable, and it is the 11px mono eyebrow style in two lines. Fix: move the phase line into the header, and demote or move the "extra session" row above the footer so only Start + nav stick. Evidence: `A-first-run/08-first-session.png`, `A-first-run/09-first-session-bottom.png`, `persona-pullup/flows/session-log-set/01-brief.png`.
3. **FIX-THIS-WEEK: the brief is long and text-first before any exercise appears.** Three "How to run this" cards of prose (about 190px) come before the first exercise, and the Follow-along cards are nested scroll regions. Each one clips at about 3 rows and sits inside the page scroll (`09-first-session-bottom.png` shows a cut-off row, "Band shoulder prep", at the sticky edge). That is a scroll-in-scroll trap on touch. Fix: show 3 rows, then "+N more"; don't clip with a hidden inner scroll. Evidence: `persona-pullup/flows/session-log-set/01-brief.png`.
4. **LATER: the Today red-state screen is mostly empty.** The rest card occupies 155-312px, and about 300px of blank space sits above the nav (`C-viewport-375/01-today-top.png`). "Log how it feels" is an underlined text link about 18px tall. It is the only action and should be 44px+, ideally a secondary button. The thumb zone is empty while the action lives in the upper third. Fix: use a full-width secondary button at the bottom of the card. The session screen's "Back to Today" button is better-sized (`03-session-top.png`), but the card text link has the same weakness there.
5. **LATER: the morning check has a sticky "Update check" button that covers the first row of severity chips.** At 375, the Shoulder card's None/Mild/Notable/Severe chips are half-hidden under the button (y 510-540), so it is easy to mis-tap the covered row (`C-viewport-375/05-check-top.png`). Fix: add bottom padding equal to the footer height (button + nav ≈ 110px) on the scroll container, or keep this CTA in-flow.
6. **LATER: the severity chips are shorter than 44px at 375.** They are about 30px tall visible (`05-check-top.png`). The 5-chip rate-effort row in the rest screen is about 66px tall, which is fine, but the labels there are 11px (`persona-pullup/flows/session-log-set/03-rest.png`), and "5+ more", "~4 more" are tiny. Type size belongs to the visual-craft reviewer.
7. **LATER: the intake Yes/No buttons are small relative to the screen.** They are about 66x46px, left-aligned, with a large void below. A Yes/No pair could be a full-width stacked pair or a 50/50 row for thumb reach; the Next button is right-aligned at the bottom, which is good for right thumbs (`A-first-run/06-intake-05.png`).
8. **LATER: the Plan header text pile.** The Plan screen has heading + a 2-line description + a progress bar + a week switcher + a "Mixed practice" card before the first day row at y~440. The day list begins at 66% of the viewport (`C-viewport-375/02-plan-top.png`). Drop the description sentence, and fold the progress bar and the "Mixed practice" card together.
9. **LATER: the program detail page is a long text wall.** "Section 1 of 4" cards of prose lead with 5-6 line paragraphs; there are no visuals and no pinned "Start" CTA at 375 before the fold (`A-first-run/05-program-detail.png`). For ad traffic, a sticky Start button on this page matters more than any other change here.

## Defect vs taste
- No horizontal overflow is reported on any C-viewport screen (`C-viewport-375/notes.md`: "horizontal overflow: false"). The sticky nav is `fixed bottom-0`, 53px high, and the bottom nav sits clear of content, with the active tab marked by a top bar plus bold white text (not colour alone).
- Items 2, 3 and 5 are defects (content covered or clipped). Items 4, 7, 8 and 9 are mostly taste/density.

## What to keep
- **The in-session set screen** (`persona-pullup/flows/session-log-set/02-set.png`): the large 10-rep dial, a 64px primary Start centred at y~770, and Back/Skip on either side. This is a textbook thumb-zone layout with every control about 56px or more. "This hurts" in the top right is reachable and clearly red, and the X to close is 44px.
- **The rest screen** (`03-rest.png`): a big 2:00 timer, effort chips in the middle third and a full-width "Skip rest" at y~686. The +30s / Skip pair is 48px tall.
- **The Record heatmap** (`C-viewport-375/04-record-top.png`): it fits at 375 with no scroll, and the legend is readable.
- **The bottom nav** at 4 items, about 94px each, with a clear active state.
- **The brand header and the new 32px H1**: wraps cleanly now that the program name is an eyebrow (cb83e26), as in `persona-pullup/flows/session-log-set/01-brief.png`.
- **Intake chrome**: a pinned Back/Next footer, a step counter and a progress bar (`06-intake-05.png`).
