Engine & endurance

· 3

Build aerobic base or specific conditioning capacity.

  • Engine Builder — Block 1: Base

    referenced

    Block 1 of a 3-block, ~6-month engine transformation. Eight weeks of Zone 1/2 base with a single weekly hard session added in week 4. Row, bike, ski-erg, or run.

    8 wk·~3-4 hr/week

  • Rowing 2K Test Prep

    referenced

    For rowers chasing the 2K PR. Six weeks, cited, tapered — no more than that.

    6 wk·~4 hr/week

  • Engine Builder — Block 2: Volume

    provisional

    Block 2 of the 3-block engine transformation. 8-12 weeks. Two hard sessions per week — a threshold cruise + a VO2max session that alternates 4×4 and short-interval formats — on top of a rising Z1 volume floor. Threshold pace shift is the headline metric.

    10 wk·~5-6 hr/week (4-5 sessions/wk, 35-105 min each)
