Overhead Mobility

activereferenced

Shoulder + thoracic + scap sequence for stronger snatch, OHS, and press. Kinematic base before load.

Levels:Foundation→Progression→Push

Adapts to you. Intake picks your tier; drill selection and progression pace adapt to your ROM. Every session sharpens further from your logs.

10 weeks·~90 min/week (daily 10-15 min)

Who this is for

You want to snatch, OHS, or press without impingement — but your arms don't reach vertical loaded. Or they do, but only barely.

What you'll achieve

By week 10: loaded shoulder flexion at 180°, empty-bar OHS to parallel or better, first snatch-grip Sotts press. Retest via goniometer + OHS depth.

Retest

Supine shoulder flexion + OHS depth ratio at phase-end (weeks 3, 7, 10).

Baseline setup — a few minutes on the wizard + a 3-day measurement window

8 short questions and 3 benchmark tests to set your baseline. The rest of the program is generated from that.
