Who this is for

You want to handstand walk. Some athletes can't hold a wall handstand yet; some walk 10m+ but can't turn; the app meets you where you are and builds each sub-capability separately.

What you'll achieve

Block 1 of a multi-block arc. Beginners: consistent wall handstand + brief freestand attempts. Wall-holders: first freestand hold + first walk step. Freestanders: 3-5 continuous walk steps. Advanced: first pirouette or obstacle traverse.

Retest

Weekly retest of freestand hold time; every 4 weeks: full assessment (wall hold, freestand, walk distance, wrist symptom score).

Baseline setup — a few minutes on the wizard + a 3-day measurement window

12 short questions and 5 benchmark tests to set your baseline. The rest of the program is generated from that.
