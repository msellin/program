Provocative positions avoided

  • Deep hip flexion + adduction + internal rotation — FADIR was positive bilaterally, left worse. Occurs under load when knees cave in a squat.
  • Lumbar extension combined with rotation — Directly loads the L5 pseudoarthrosis.
  • Loaded end-range hip flexion, especially eccentric — Clicking documented in the lowering phase of hanging leg raises.
  • Resisted straight-leg raise, left — Reproduces groin pain. Documented twice, a year apart.
  • Heavy axial spinal load (back squat, conventional deadlift) — The original presenting complaint — could not stand up from a deadlift due to sharp pain.

Red-flag rules

  • Pain that wakes you at night — action: escalate to clinician
  • Sharp catch or click WITH pain in the groin — action: back off two steps
  • Deep dull ache in left buttock / SI area lasting over 48h — action: back off two steps
  • Pain that shortens your stride when running — action: stop running session
  • New weakness in shoulder external rotation, or scapular asymmetry — action: escalate to clinician
  • Morning stiffness over 30 min, back pain improving with movement and worsening with rest, second-half-of-night waking — action: escalate to clinician
