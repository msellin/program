Anterior hip + strength rebuild

PERSONAL

For lifters with a history of anterior-hip / groin irritation who want to rebuild squat and deadlift base without provoking symptoms.

Authored for one specific clinical context. Not marketed as an evidence-backed catalog program — the “why” lives in the author's clinical notes, not in a public references list. Use only if your situation resembles the author's.

SECTION 1 OF 4

Who this is for

You've had episodes of hip flexor / groin pain that flared under squatting or Metcons. You want to keep training seriously — not stop. This program is your barbell arc built around what your hip can actually take.

SECTION 2 OF 4

What you'll achieve

A 12-month strength arc with structured re-introduction, three progressive 5/3/1 cycles, a Hatch specialisation block, and a peak test. Provocative positions are respected throughout. Ends with a specialist-ready report of your symptom + load history.

SECTION 3 OF 4

What it takes
34 weeks · ~5 hr/week
Difficulty · intermediate

SECTION 4 OF 4

How we prove it works

Cycle-end 5-rep max on primary lifts (squat, block pull) with symptom review. Every 4 weeks.
